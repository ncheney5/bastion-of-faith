Part 5 of 6 · Genesis 39:12

Respond

Leave your garment and flee.

“And he left his garment in her hand, and fled, and got him out.”
Genesis 39:12

An action plan was set earlier. Now that the attack has been detected, this is where you act. You remove the influences that need to go, and you remove yourself from the threat.

A child who tells a parent has done exactly the right thing. Respond with gratitude, not punishment, so they will come to you again.

In the NIST framework

Act on a detected attack to contain it.

On the home network

Disconnect a compromised device, change passwords, block the source, and report scams.

Activities for every age

Three versions of this part — pick the one that fits who you’re teaching.

Activity · Kids

Joseph’s Run

  1. Read Genesis 39:12 and talk about how fast Joseph left.
  2. Make scenario cards, like “A friend shows you something on their phone.”
  3. Take turns drawing a card and acting out how to flee.
  4. Make the family rule clear: telling a parent is always rewarded, never punished.

Scenario cards

A friend shows you something on their phone that you know is wrong.
A message says you won a prize and asks for Mom’s password.
A game chat starts asking where you live.

Activity · Teens

The Script

  1. You won’t think clearly in the moment — so write the plan now.
  2. For each situation below, write exactly what you’d do and say.
  3. Settle your “first ten seconds” move and who you’d tell.
  4. Keep it somewhere you’ll remember it.

Write your move

A friend sends something harmful in the group chat.
Someone pressures you for a photo.
You stumble onto something you can’t unsee.

Activity · Adults

Family Response Plan

  1. Decide the exact steps a family member takes when something bad happens: stop, close or disconnect, tell the safe person.
  2. Name the “safe person,” and make the rule explicit — coming forward is always rewarded, never punished.
  3. Cover the practical moves too: change a password, disconnect a device, block and report.
  4. Write it down and make sure everyone knows it by heart.

Our response plan

Safe personEither parent — no punishment, ever.
DeviceDisconnect and change the password.
ReportBlock, screenshot, and tell.

Ponder

  • What will I do in the first ten seconds?
  • Who will I tell, and how will they respond?
