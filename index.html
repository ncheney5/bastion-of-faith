A Bastion of Faith

Guarding the home with Captain Moroni and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework

“I teach them correct principles, and they govern themselves.”

Joseph Smith

Filters and network monitoring matter, and this guide covers them. But every wall can be climbed. The stronger defense is a child who knows what is true, recognizes when something is wrong, and already knows what to do. Captain Moroni built forts and towers, and he also prepared the minds of his people. The six functions of the NIST framework give that same work a structure a family can follow.
